One-time setup in the Shortcuts app

  1. Open the Shortcuts app and tap + (top right).
  2. Tap the name at the top, choose Rename, and type exactly: Baby Companion Alarm
  3. Tap Search Actions, type Get Dates from Input, and tap it.
  4. In that action, tap the blue word Input and choose Shortcut Input. (If a line saying "Receive input from Nowhere" appears at the top, that's fine.)
  5. Search for Create Alarm (a Clock action) and tap it.
  6. In Create Alarm, tap the time and pick the Dates variable from step 3. Optionally set the label to Feed time.
  7. Tap Done. Come back here and tap Test: alarm in 2 min. When iOS asks, tap Always Allow.

iPhone alarms ring even on silent and when the phone is locked. One-time alarms switch off after ringing; swipe old ones away in the Clock app now and then.

The alarm button works on iPhone. On this device, set an alarm in your Clock app.
